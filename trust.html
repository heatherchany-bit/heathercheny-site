Fees & rules

Clear fees. Clear rules.

Our fee

We charge a fee for finding your item. The fee is explained to you and agreed in writing before the search begins. There are no hidden charges.

Your privacy

We keep your request and your details private. We only share what is needed to find and buy the item.

What we do not source

  • Stolen goods
  • Products from protected wildlife, for example ivory
  • Anything that is illegal to buy, sell or send in the countries involved
  • Fakes sold as real

If a request does not meet these rules, we say no. We may ask for papers or proof before we buy an item.

Requests are for adults, 18 or older.

Questions?

Ask us before you decide. There is no pressure.
